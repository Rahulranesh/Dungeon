This Privacy Policy explains what information Solo Levelling Dungeon (“the Game”, “we”, “us”) does and does not collect, how the Game handles your data, and the choices you have. We designed the Game to be private by default: it works fully offline, requires no account, contains no advertising, and includes no analytics or tracking SDKs.

The short version: we do not collect, sell, or share your personal data. Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device. The only data that ever reaches us is what you choose to send us yourself (for example, a support email).

1. Scope of this policy

This policy applies to the Solo Levelling Dungeon application distributed through Google Play, the Apple App Store, and other official channels, and to this website. It does not apply to third-party services you may use alongside the Game (see Section 8), which are governed by their own privacy policies.

2. Information we do not collect

We do not collect or have access to any of the following:

  • Personal identifiers such as your name, email address, phone number, or postal address (unless you email us first);
  • Account or login credentials — the Game has no accounts or sign-in;
  • Precise or approximate location (no GPS, no location permissions);
  • Advertising identifiers, and no advertising or behavioral profiling of any kind;
  • Contacts, photos, files, calendar, microphone, camera, or health data;
  • Analytics, telemetry, or crash-reporting data transmitted automatically. The Game includes no third-party analytics, advertising, or automatic crash-reporting SDKs.

3. Data stored on your device

Your game data — save files, run history, high scores, badges, preferences, and settings — is stored locally in the Game’s private storage on your device. It is not uploaded to us or to any server we control. If you uninstall the Game, this local data is removed with it (subject to any device or platform backups you have configured).

The Game may also write technical logs locally on your device (for example, error messages for troubleshooting). These logs stay on your device and are only sent to us if you deliberately include them in a support email.

4. Network connections

The Game normally works fully offline. When an internet connection is available, it may make standard HTTPS requests for two optional features:

  • Update checks — to see whether a newer version of the Game or a related announcement is available;
  • In-game news — to display news and changelog content.

These requests are sent to content-hosting services such as GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com / github.com). They contain no personal identifiers, game data, or advertising IDs. As with any internet request, the receiving service can see technical information such as your IP address and user agent, and may keep standard server logs under its own privacy policy. We do not receive, store, or combine that information.

5. Device and platform backups

If you have enabled operating-system backups (for example, Android Backup or iCloud), your device may include the Game’s local data in those backups. These backups are controlled by you and your platform provider; we have no access to them and receive no copy of your data.

6. Support communications

If you contact us (for example, by email at berryloopofficial@gmail.com or by opening an issue on our public project page), we receive whatever information you choose to include — typically your email address, message, and any attachments such as screenshots or log files. We use it only to respond to you and resolve the issue, and we do not add you to marketing lists or share it with third parties for their own purposes. You may ask us to delete this correspondence at any time.

7. Purchases and platform services

If you download or (where applicable) purchase the Game through an app store, the store processes the transaction, payment details, and store account information. We do not receive your payment details. Store operators may also collect their own diagnostic and usage data under their own policies. The Game itself does not contain in-app purchases, subscriptions, or advertising.

8. Third-party services

The only third parties involved in operating the Game are:

  • App stores (Google Play, Apple App Store) — distribution and, if applicable, payments;
  • Content hosting (GitHub and similar static hosting) — serves update and news information;
  • Firebase Hosting (Google) — serves this policy and terms website. Like any web host, Firebase processes standard technical logs (such as IP address and user agent) to deliver pages and prevent abuse.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes. No third-party advertising networks, data brokers, or analytics providers are integrated into the Game.

9. Children’s privacy

The Game is a general-audience game that is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area, or the equivalent minimum age in your country). Because the Game has no accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases, and no data collection, it does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under GDPR or CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal data. Because we do not collect or maintain personal data through the Game, in most cases there is nothing for us to retrieve or delete — uninstalling the Game removes its local data. If you have sent us correspondence and would like it deleted, email us and we will act on your request within 30 days. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

11. Data retention

We retain support correspondence only for as long as needed to resolve your issue and for reasonable record-keeping afterward, after which it is deleted. We hold no other personal data about you.

12. Security

The Game’s network requests use HTTPS. We keep our build and release tooling up to date and limit access to any correspondence we receive. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

13. International users

The Game is available worldwide and stores your game data locally on your own device. If you email us, your message will be processed where we operate, with appropriate safeguards for cross-border transfers where required by law.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example to reflect new features or legal requirements. The updated version will always be posted at this URL with a new effective date, and material changes will be highlighted in the Game’s changelog or news feed. Continued use of the Game after an update means you accept the revised policy.
